Globbler Guide

Picture-led walkthrough for the Globbler unlock: lights and the scorched transponder on Gravity Farms (Singularity), then claim on Ground Zero.

At a glance

  1. Gravity Farms (Singularity) — setup
  2. Mushroom light
  3. Tower light
  4. Factory / building light
  5. Scorched transponder
  6. Ground Zero — claim Globbler

How to use this guide

  • Start from the overview map so the numbered / marked spots line up before you roam.
  • Each light has its own detail shot — use those when the overview pin is too small on a phone.
  • Singularity keeps a stable layout; the mission-select example shows the permanent-map style.

2. Mushroom light

Area: Gravity Farms (Singularity)

Area: Gravity Farms (Singularity)

  • Clear the factory / building light (and the related room if you are still missing a trigger).

5. Scorched transponder

Area: Gravity Farms (Singularity)

  • Interact with the scorched transponder after the lights are done.

6. Ground Zero — claim Globbler

Mission select: Ground Zero

  • Launch Ground Zero.
  • Claim Globbler when the objective allows.

Notes

  • Light order on this page matches a clean route; if one is already lit from a prior run, skip ahead.
  • Singularity map art is stable — if a still disagrees with your client after a patch, prefer the live objective marker.
  • Images live in Mycopunk-Abomination-Image-Repo under globbler_guide_images/.
