About

Personal testing of Mycopunk’s relative balance. Build pages are ranked by kill time on the Amalgamation boss at Threat 6 — the tier the game is balanced around. This is my own testing, so coverage is not perfectly even across every weapon.

How to read the data

  • Target: Amalgamation on Threat 6.
  • Metric: kill time from the exact frame the boss spawns (mod-timed).
  • Setup counts: charge and setup time are included in the kill time, so long wind-ups are not free wins on paper.
  • Tool-assist: some entries use tool-assisted execution — notably Livewire-based builds and Gunship Cannon builds. Treat those times as assisted ceilings, not typical unassisted play.
  • Build pages: loadouts ranked by that kill time; tiers follow the time distribution.
  • Weapon tierlist: not the same dataset. It is feel-based for the general game, with a bias toward weapons that both horde clear and boss.
  • Bias: personal playtime — weapons I dislike are probably under-tested.
