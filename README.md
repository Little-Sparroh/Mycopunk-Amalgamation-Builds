Knowledge to Give
-----------------
Weapon Combos:

Most players will opt for the Primary + Secondary approach to weapon combos.
The best secondaries in the game are: Plate Launcher, Swarm Launcher, Shocklance.
An honorable meantion to Globbler secondary.
This can also be circumvented for a dual primary approach.
This is possible since dealing damage with one weapon regenerates ammo for the other.

Element:

When choosing an element your weapon comes first
What elements are available?
For Trident we have the base (Normal), Fire, and Acid.
We then choose our grenade based on the element chosen. (normal is generally weaker)
In my case I will generally choose Fire if available, then Shock, then Acid in that order.
Photon Disc can be used to substitute for any element but it comes with drawbacks and unique advantages.

Employee:

What employee you choose will also change some aspects of how you approach the game.
Generally, all employees will be fine to play with niches they each fill.
Wrangler has very fast movement with good 3D navigation.
Bruiser has good movement that depends on specific upgrades and player skill.
Scrapper has the easiest vertical movement.
Glider has the easiest horizontal movement.
Some builds are dependant or get better with certain employees.
I wouldnt want to use Gunship Cannon with the Gunship upgrade while playing bruiser.
I also would want to increase my damage on the Accelerator so I would likely play Wrangler.
I would also want to get the most out of my Carver so I would choose Scrapper.

Heavy Weapon:

Heavy weapons are not very good generally even with the upgrades.

New Rules:

Heavy Weapons are actually viable now as tools

Grenades are a bit better spread now. Fire is still best, Shock is dependant upon weapon, Acid is based on essentially IF YOU HAVE NOTHING ELSE, Photon is great for Normal Damage or if you need ammo regen / multi element support, Snowball is used purely for cryo.

Mycologist has the most complex gameplay but great overall movement.
