## Knowledge to Give

### Weapon Combos
Most players use a Primary + Secondary setup.  
The best secondaries are **Plate Launcher**, **Swarm Launcher**, and **Shocklance**. Globbler is an honorable mention.

A dual-primary approach is also viable because dealing damage with one weapon regenerates ammo for the other.

### Element
Choose your weapon element first.  
Trident has Normal, Fire, and Acid. Grenade choice follows the element (Normal is generally weaker).

Personal priority: **Fire > Shock > Acid**.  
Photon Disc can substitute for any element but comes with its own drawbacks and advantages.

### Employee
All employees are viable and fill different niches:

- **Wrangler** — Very fast movement with strong 3D navigation
- **Bruiser** — Good movement that depends on specific upgrades and player skill
- **Scrapper** — Easiest vertical movement
- **Glider** — Easiest horizontal movement
- **Mycologist** — Has the most complex gameplay but offers great overall movement.

Some builds prefer certain employees:
- Gunship Cannon + Gunship upgrade is not ideal on Bruiser
- Accelerator damage scaling favors Wrangler
- Carver gets the most out of Scrapper

### Heavy Weapons
Heavy weapons are generally weak even with upgrades.  
They are more usable now as tools rather than main damage sources.

### Grenades
Fire is still the strongest overall.  
Shock is weapon-dependent. Acid is mostly a last resort.  
Photon is excellent for Normal damage, ammo regen, or multi-element support.  
Snowball is used purely for cryo which is niche.
