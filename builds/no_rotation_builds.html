Accelerator – Inertia Accelerant

Kill Time: —

Secondary: Swarm Launcher

Grenade: Voltaic Grenade

Elemental Cocktail: Shock to Acid

Comments: All Accelerator builds have the same goal of "how many rockets can i shoot". This build is the basic implementation of that. Just raw damage increase and volume.

Accelerator – Requeening

Kill Time: —

Secondary: Swarm Launcher

Grenade: Voltaic Grenade

Elemental Cocktail: Shock to Acid

Comments: The requeening build ends up fun but not a showstopper. Since we now have the tools to make this build work it can stretch its legs. Unfortunately its just not as good scaling as just stacking more speed multipliers.

Accelerator – Ruptured Spleen

Kill Time: —

Secondary: Swarm Launcher

Grenade: Voltaic Grenade

Elemental Cocktail: Shock to Acid

Comments: This build sacrifices volume of missiles for a very heavy missile. It functions pretty alright in bossing, but id wonder if it even really works in horde clear due to the nature of how it reloads. If you miss too many bullets you just fail to be able to regenerate your ammo.

Beam Ripper – Inverted Spear of Hell

Grenade: Caustic Grenade

Elemental Cocktail: Shock to Acid

Kill Time: —

Secondary: Shocklance

Grenade: Caustic Grenade

Diffraction Driver – Capacity Waiver

Kill Time: —

Secondary: Swarm Launcher

Grenade: Incendiary Grenade

Elemental Cocktail: Fire to Acid

Comments: Diffraction Driver is nowhere near the top of the power charts, but it is a solid weapon. Its ceiling isnt that great but it, much like the Cycler, relies on Internal Combustion to offset its lack of damage.

Diffraction Driver – Cryogenic Firing Chamber

Kill Time: —

Secondary: Swarm Launcher

Grenade: Snowball

Comments: This build is a little different. Since it is a cryo build and we dont have many on the list, it having Snowball means that a lot of our damage is actually coming from Shardburst. Its not horrible as a concept, but is very specifically ok, since the Snowball lacks healing options.

Diffraction Driver – Pocket Sniper
